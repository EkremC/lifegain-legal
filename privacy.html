Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Your Privacy Matters

LifeGain is designed with privacy at its core. This policy explains in simple terms what data we collect, how we use it, and how you maintain control over your information.

Who LifeGain Is For

LifeGain is intended for people aged 18 and older. It is not intended for use by anyone under 18.

What Data We Collect

Personal Information:

  • Profile name, age, weight, and height; age and BMI are used for microlife estimates
  • Activity logs (workouts, duration, intensity)
  • Personal goals and preferences

Optional Data:

  • Apple Health workouts, steps, and active energy (only if you choose to connect)
  • Notification preferences

We DO NOT collect:

  • Location data
  • Contacts or photos
  • Payment-card details
  • Any data for advertising

How We Use Your Data

We use information to:

  • Calculate personalized microlife estimates
  • Show your activity history, progress, and insights
  • Sync saved records across your devices through iCloud
  • Manage purchases, restore subscriptions, and provide Pro access when subscription services are enabled

We do not sell your personal data or use your health and activity records for advertising. We use the service providers described below to provide synchronization and subscription features.

Why We Process Information

Personalized Health-Related Processing:

We rely on your consent to use your age, height, weight, BMI, and activity information for personalized calculations and progress tracking (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). Where this information qualifies as health data, we rely on your explicit consent under Article 9(2)(a). The onboarding agreement describes these uses and the possible synchronization of saved records through your iCloud account. Connecting Apple Health is a separate, optional choice.

Subscription Access:

When subscription services are enabled, we process the purchase and subscription information necessary to provide the Pro access you request, restore purchases, and manage access under our service terms (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). This does not include a blanket contractual basis for advertising, unrelated analytics, or other optional uses.

See Your Data Rights below for how to withdraw your health-data agreement and what happens to saved records.

Where Your Data Is Stored

Profile and Activity Records:

  • Your profile and activity records are stored on your device using Core Data
  • These records may also sync through your iCloud account when iCloud is available for LifeGain

iCloud Storage:

  • iCloud storage is managed by Apple and is subject to Apple's security protections and your account settings

Subscription Services:

  • When enabled, subscription services process purchase information separately from your profile and activity records, as described below

Subscriptions & RevenueCat

When subscription services are enabled, LifeGain uses RevenueCat to manage purchases, restore purchases, and determine access to Pro features.

RevenueCat processes purchase history, subscription status, a randomly generated app user identifier, and technical information needed to provide these services. Purchase information is used for subscription functionality and subscription analytics, not advertising tracking.

LifeGain does not send your profile name, age, height, weight, BMI, activity records, or Apple Health summaries to RevenueCat. LifeGain does not receive your payment-card details.

How Long Records Are Kept

Profile and Activity History:

LifeGain keeps saved profile and activity records to provide your ongoing history and progress. The app does not automatically delete these records after a period of inactivity. You can delete individual activities or your active profile and its activities using the controls described below.

iCloud and Other Copies:

Deletions may sync through iCloud; they are not necessarily immediate across devices. Exported files and original Apple Health records remain outside LifeGain's profile-deletion controls. Uninstalling LifeGain does not request deletion of iCloud or subscription-service records.

Subscription Records:

Ending a subscription does not automatically erase purchase history. Apple and RevenueCat process subscription records separately from your activity history. Deleting your LifeGain profile does not delete those subscription records.

Your Data Rights

Where applicable, data-protection law gives you rights to access, correct, erase, or receive a portable copy of your personal data, and to restrict or object to certain processing. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing. These rights are subject to the conditions and exceptions in applicable law.

You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

The app provides the following controls for saved LifeGain records:

  • Export: Export saved profile and activity data
  • Delete: Delete your active LifeGain profile and its activities
  • Modify: Edit your profile information
  • Permissions: Manage Apple Health access in Apple Health settings

To delete your profile, go to Profile → Settings → Data Management → Delete Profile and Activities. Deletions may sync through iCloud.

To withdraw your health-data agreement on this device, open Settings → Data Management → Health Data Consent. You can export first. After the final deletion confirmation succeeds, LifeGain deletes your active profile and its activities and returns to onboarding. New Apple Health reads and personalized calculations on this device are blocked until you agree again. This does not revoke Apple Health permissions or switch off another device; iCloud deletions may take time.

Deleting your profile does not delete original Apple Health records, exported copies, or purchase records held by subscription services. It does not cancel a subscription.

For subscriptions purchased through the App Store, manage or cancel them in iPhone Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions.

HealthKit Integration

If you choose to connect HealthKit:

  • We request read access to workouts, steps, and active energy
  • We import workouts from the last seven days during setup and Health Insights refresh
  • Imported records include the workout identifier, start date, duration, mapped activity type, estimated intensity, and LifeGain's calculated gains
  • Workout active energy, when available, helps estimate intensity; the original calorie value is not saved in the activity record
  • Step and daily active energy summaries are computed on your device for Health Insights and held in memory, not saved to LifeGain's activity database
  • Imported activity records are saved in LifeGain and may sync through your iCloud account
  • LifeGain does not request or read heart-rate data
  • LifeGain does not write data to Apple Health
  • Manage permissions in Apple Health settings; revoking access does not delete earlier imports

Visiting Our Legal Pages

When you visit a LifeGain legal page hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs and stores your IP address for security purposes, even if you are not signed into GitHub.

These static legal pages do not include custom analytics, advertising trackers, forms, or external fonts. Opening a legal page does not send your LifeGain profile or activity history to the website.

GitHub's Privacy Statement explains its handling of website visitor information: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement
