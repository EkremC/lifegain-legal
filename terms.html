Scientific Calculations

The microlife concept was described by David Spiegelhalter of the University of Cambridge in published work, including a 2012 BMJ article.

LifeGain uses its own activity-tracking calculation, with activity type, duration, intensity, age, and BMI as inputs. Its activity rates are inspired by physical-activity energy references and adjusted for product consistency.

These sources do not validate LifeGain's individual estimates or imply endorsement by the authors or their institutions.

Important limitations:

  • Displayed values are educational tracking estimates, not personal lifespan predictions
  • Individual results may vary significantly
  • Many factors affect longevity beyond exercise
  • Past performance does not guarantee future results
